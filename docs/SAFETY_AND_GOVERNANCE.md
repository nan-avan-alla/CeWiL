# Safety and Governance

## Human approval
The platform may organise information, route requests, and produce draft calculations or documentation. It must not independently certify structural adequacy, approve designs, issue statutory compliance determinations, or authorise construction.

## Evidence expectations
Future workflows should retain source documents, revision identifiers, assumptions, units, calculation methods, reviewer identity, review date, and approval status.

## Jurisdiction awareness
Building codes, standards, municipal rules, and design loads vary by jurisdiction and revision. Any future code-aware feature must identify the applicable jurisdiction and source edition and must surface uncertainty.

## Calculation controls
Deterministic calculation services should validate units and input ranges, expose formulas, and be tested against independently checked examples. Engineering calculations require qualified review before use.

## Data protection
Do not commit personal data, client drawings, credentials, API keys, or confidential project records. Use managed secrets and access controls when integrations are introduced.
